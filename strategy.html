2. Strategy & risk

Long-only momentum: 20-bar moving average above 50-bar average, close above previous 20-bar high. Signal at close, entry at next bar open.

Maximum one open position; capped at cash notional, no leverage. Stop wins if both stop and target are hit within the same bar. Open gaps may worsen the exit. No overnight financing model.
